How to add it to your email

Outlook (desktop, new or classic)

  1. Click Copy signature.
  2. Outlook: File > Options > Mail > Signatures (new Outlook: Settings > Accounts > Signatures).
  3. Click New, give it a name, click in the editor box and press Ctrl+V.
  4. Choose it as the default for new messages and replies, then Save.

Outlook on the web

  1. Settings > Account > Signatures, then paste with Ctrl+V and Save.

Gmail

  1. Settings (gear) > See all settings > General > Signature.
  2. Create new, paste with Ctrl+V, scroll down and click Save Changes.
